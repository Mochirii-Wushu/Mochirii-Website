# Legal and Privacy Readiness Decision Packet

Date: 2026-07-29

Status: source-only factual draft

Authority: none for legal advice, public-copy changes, provider changes, deployment, or launch

This packet records the smallest public-safe legal and privacy decision surface
that can be established from reviewed repository source. It is not legal
advice, does not establish a legal basis, and does not prove current provider
or runtime state.

The machine-readable source of truth is
[legal-privacy-readiness.v1.json](./legal-privacy-readiness.v1.json). Its
validator accepts explicit blockers as honest state; it does not convert a
missing decision into readiness.

## Fact boundary

- **SOURCE_OBSERVED** means only that reviewed repository source contains the
  statement.
- **RUNTIME_READBACK_REQUIRED** means a separately authorized hosted or
  operational readback is needed.
- **BLOCKED_APPROVAL** means an accountable owner or qualified reviewer must
  decide and approve the fact or wording.
- **BLOCKED_EXTERNAL** means current professional, contractual, provider, or
  runtime evidence is required.
- **DEFERRED_BY_EXPLICIT_POLICY** means the surface must remain inactive.
- Restricted contracts, counsel communications, personal data, credentials,
  private identifiers, provider exports, and recovery material remain outside
  public Git.

No Website, Social, storefront, raffle, mobile, game, provider, or database
state changes follow from this packet.

## Operator decisions required

The release owner must approve factual answers before counsel or public copy
uses them:

1. Legal operator or controller, entity type, formation and registration
   jurisdiction, principal operating location, and exact trade-name authority.
2. Public postal address, phone, privacy contact, representative, responsible
   person, incident owner, support owner, and security owner.
3. Intended visitor and member jurisdictions, launch regions, languages, age
   thresholds, and whether Social parental controls remain permanently
   retired.
4. Purpose-by-purpose legal bases and any consent-dependent workflows.
5. Retention periods, deletion triggers, backup horizons, restore handling,
   immutable-evidence rules, and legal holds.
6. Rights intake, identity verification, deadlines, appeals, authorized
   agents, non-retaliation, provider propagation, and proof returned to the
   requester.
7. One source-of-truth architecture for terms, privacy, community guidelines,
   legal notices, deletion guidance, and versioned assent evidence.
8. Counsel scope, cost, reviewers, privileged evidence boundary, and the
   public-safe approval receipt.

Displaying event or raffle times in UTC+8 does not establish a legal
jurisdiction.

## Counsel packet

Before a public legal-copy change, prepare a private packet containing:

- approved operator and jurisdiction facts;
- the machine processing, subprocessor, retention, and rights matrices;
- current provider contracts and data-flow evidence;
- cookie, SDK, pixel, analytics, log, and telemetry inventory;
- user-generated-content, copyright, publicity, moderation, appeal, law
  enforcement, and incident workflows;
- raffle sponsor, country, no-purchase, tax, reward, and funding records;
- storefront policy, privacy, tax, safety, complaint, and fulfillment records;
- age, child-safety, mobile, game, and platform applicability;
- proposed redlines with claim-to-evidence mapping; and
- the requested review jurisdictions, scope, cost, and reviewer qualifications.

Retain privileged, personal, contractual, and private business evidence only in
the approved restricted boundary. Track only public-safe review and approval
references in Git.

## Release gates

Public legal copy may change only after:

1. the inventory has no unreviewed gap for the affected surface;
2. current provider and runtime facts are read back under exact authorization;
3. qualified counsel reviews the applicable jurisdiction and subject matter;
4. the release owner approves the exact rendered copy;
5. source, build, rendered output, effective date, version, and hash are bound;
6. rights, deletion, consent, and provider-propagation workflows pass; and
7. the ordinary protected-branch, preview, deployment, rollback, and
   post-release checks are separately approved.

Raffle, storefront, mobile, game, provider activation, secrets, paid services,
and public launch retain their own stricter gates.
